# Research sources

Accessed 11 August 2026 unless stated otherwise. Primary government, regulator, platform, and vendor documentation is preferred. Vendor adoption claims are treated as directional, not audited facts.

## Market and digitisation

- [Ministry of MSME Annual Report 2025–26](https://msme.gov.in/sites/default/files/MSMEANNUALREPORT2025-26ENGLISH_0.pdf) — registrations, delayed-payment framework, and MSME programmes.
- [MSME performance dashboard](https://dashboard.msme.gov.in/dashboard.aspx) — current Udyam/UAP counts and enterprise distribution.
- [Government assessment of MSME digital adoption and ONDC participation](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2236872&lang=1&reg=3) — 206,000 merchants with at least one ONDC transaction as of 31 December 2025.
- [Vi Business MSME Growth Insights Study 2026 release](https://www.myvi.in/content/dam/vodafoneideadigital/business/Press-Releases/2023/Vi-Business-MSME-Growth-Insights-Study-57-MSMEs-view-AI-as-a-Vital-Scale-Driver.pdf) — 57% view AI as a growth tool and 25% report adoption; based on the ReadyForNext platform cohort.
- [Transforming Small Businesses: An AI Playbook for India’s SMEs](https://www.psa.gov.in/CMS/web/sites/default/files/publication/WEF_Transforming_Small_Businesses_2025.pdf) — Government/WEF/BCG analysis of AI opportunities, barriers, and cluster-based adoption.
- [India SME Forum Digitalisation Report Card 2025](https://indiasmeforum.org/digishaastra/assets/docs/Final-META-Report-Card-2025.pdf) — indicative survey of 7,835 MSMEs; convenience sample, not nationally representative.
- [Meta: Business AI for small businesses in India](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/05/introducing-business-ai-on-whatsapp-for-small-businesses-in-india/amp/) — product direction and Kantar claim that 91% of online Indian adults chat with a business weekly.

## GST and business records

- [GST Council newsletter, June 2025](https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-07/newsletter_june_2025_0.pdf) — 1.518 crore active GST registrations as of 30 April 2025.
- [CBIC tax invoice rules](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/gst-invoice-rules.html) — mandatory invoice fields.
- [GST e-invoice overview](https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/downloads/news/pamphlet_e_invoice_overview_updated_on_17_08_2023_approved_final.pdf) — ₹5 crore applicability threshold from 1 August 2023.
- [CBIC e-way bill rules](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/ewaybill-rules.html) — general ₹50,000 consignment trigger, subject to rules and exceptions.
- [CBIC accounts and records rules](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/accnt-record-rules.html) — electronic edit/delete logs, backups, stock records, and audit inter-linkages.
- [CGST Act, accounts retention](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/hindi/CGST-bill-e.html) — 72-month record-retention rule and related requirements.
- [GST taxpayer welcome kit and GSP model](https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/downloads/news/welcome_kit_for_new_taxpyers.pdf) — how third-party applications connect through GST Suvidha Providers.
- [IRIS IRP API integration overview](https://einvoice6.gst.gov.in/content/e-invoice-apis-for-solution-providers/) — sandbox and API path for solution providers.
